City

Rebuild any real address from buildings to streets, water, and trees so you can walk it in first person. The city is populated with artificial people who have two-lobe spiking neural network brains. They learn from their own hunger, thirst, and health instead of following scripts, to see what behavior emerges when real places meet adaptive minds.

Select a city from the list or enter a custom address. Buildings within a few blocks are pulled from OpenStreetMap and extruded.

WASD to move, mouse to look, shift to run, space to jump, esc to return here.
