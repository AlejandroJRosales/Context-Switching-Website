City

Rebuild any real city and its buildings, streets, water, hills, live local weather and time of day. Step inside any building, and find real grocery stores, hospitals, and shops. The city is populated with artificial people, who have two-lobe spiking neural network brains that learn from their own hunger, thirst, and health, and deer that follow simple survival rules. You also have a car.

Select a city from the list or enter a custom address. Buildings within a few blocks are pulled from OpenStreetMap and extruded.

Build Options

    WASD to move, mouse to look, shift to run, space to jump, E to enter or leave the car (space is its handbrake), esc for settings.
