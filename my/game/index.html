Nova Orbis

A procedurally generated ecosystem of fields, mountains, lakes, and plants, where people roam, eat, drink, and reproduce with no scripted behaviors, alongside coyotes and deer that follow survival rules. People's brains let them explore, experience the world, make choices, and learn. You also have a cat companion named Nibbler.

Simulate any real city in the US with its buildings, streets, water, terrain, and live local weather and time of day. Walk into real grocery stores, hospitals, and shops. The city is populated with people who have two-lobe spiking neural network brains that learn from hunger, thirst, health, and deer that follow survival rules. You also have a car.

A real US city at the heart of a procedural wilderness. Its people run on spiking neural networks with evolving topology: they sense the world, feel hunger, thirst, and injury, and learn how to respond. Coyotes stalk deer, people hunt both, and every species needs water. Each newborn inherits its parents' traits with small mutations, so the survivors shape the next generation. Your cat Nibbler rides along, and a car is waiting when you want to cover ground. Switch on Survival mode to take your own place in the ecosystem and live by the same rules as everyone else.

Select a city from the list or enter a custom address. Buildings within a few blocks are pulled from OpenStreetMap.
