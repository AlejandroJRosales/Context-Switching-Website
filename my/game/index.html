Nova Orbis

A procedurally generated ecosystem of fields, mountains, lakes, and plants, where people roam, eat, drink, and reproduce with no scripted behaviors, alongside coyotes and deer that follow survival rules. People's brains let them explore, experience the world, make choices, and learn. You also have a cat companion named Nibbler.

Simulate any real city in the US with its buildings, streets, water, terrain, and live local weather and time of day. Walk into real grocery stores, hospitals, and shops. The city is populated with people who have two-lobe spiking neural network brains that learn from hunger, thirst, health, and deer that follow survival rules. You also have a car.

Choose any US city by address to replicate at the center of a procedurally generated wilderness. People populate the ecosystem with two-lobed, spiking brains with dopamine-modulated neural plasticity. They sense the world, hunger, thirst, explore, and learn. Each newborn (coyote, deer, or person) inherits its parents' brain structure and physical traits with small mutations, so survivors shape the next generation. You have a car and a cat companion to care for. Watch the ecosystem grow organically, or switch on Survivor to live in it.
